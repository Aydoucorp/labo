# Blocs de prompt (recopier verbatim, ne changer que les trous)

## ENV (fin de chaque prompt still)
" The environment is exactly the world in the attached background reference image: keep its colours, gradient, grid, horizon height and lighting unchanged. This world is an infinite canvas: it continues unbroken past every edge of the frame, and the camera is inside it. No ceiling, no roof structure, no walls, and no platform edge or visible boundary where the ground ends. The grid is an environmental overlay with no thickness, no edges and no physical surface, not a platform or slab; everything rests naturally with a soft contact shadow. Pixar-style friendly stylized 3D, rounded forms, soft even lighting, one subject, clean negative space, must read on a phone in one second. Portrait 9:16. No text, no captions, no labels."

## Ouverture (ordre des refs = plate, PRODUIT, perso, bloc)
"Attach references: the first attached image is the background world." + si produit : " The SECOND attached image is the product photo: the product in this scene must be an exact photo-accurate copy of it, same shape, lid, label with the exact same text layout, brand name clearly legible, label facing the camera."

## Verrous
- Perso : " The man/woman is exactly the character in the attached character sheet: same face, hair, <barbe>, <vêtements>, same rendering." (+ « WITHOUT the cap » si besoin, répété au négatif)
- Bloc coupe : " The cross-section is the same stylized block as in the attached cross-section reference: <description courte du bloc>."

## NÉGATIF
"No text, no captions, no labels, no UI, no platform, no stage set, no ceiling, no walls, no dramatic lighting, no photographic noise, no photorealism on characters, no extra characters, no floating objects." (+ " No blank label, no unbranded jar." sur les stills produit ; + " No cap, no hat." quand il n'en faut pas)

## Plate (si régénération, text-to-image)
"An empty background plate, no subject at all: a bright cyan-blue world seen from a standing camera at eye level, an infinite smooth ground in the same bright cyan-blue meeting a very slightly lighter blue sky at a soft horizon around the lower third, with thin light-cyan square grid lines drawn on the ground and continuing faintly on the back wall of the world as an abstract overlay with no thickness and no edges. The world continues unbroken past every edge of the frame; no ceiling, no walls, no platform edge or visible boundary where the ground ends. Soft even lighting, no shadows, no props, no objects, no text. Portrait 9:16."

## Character sheet (image-to-image depuis un buste validé + plate)
"Character sheet of the SAME person as in the attached reference: identical face, <cheveux/barbe>, <haut>, now shown FULL BODY from head to feet, standing in a relaxed neutral pose facing the camera, arms loosely at his/her sides, wearing <bas> and <chaussures>. Pixar-style friendly stylized 3D character, rounded forms, soft even lighting, smooth matte-to-satin surfaces, no pores." + ENV. Centré, ~80 % de la hauteur.

## Leg (Kling)
"Camera motion: <UN mouvement continu, qui finit sur le cadrage de l'image de fin>. Subject motion: <ce qui bouge dans le cadre, état de départ → état d'arrivée>. One continuous camera move, smooth and physical, no cuts. Pixar-style stylized 3D in a bright cyan-blue grid world. No captions. No text."
Transitions qui marchent : « pushes forward fast into <X> and dives through <Y>, ending on … of the end frame » · « pulls back fast and drops to ground level, ending on the wide prop island » · « orbits a quarter turn … then rises to the dramatic top-down view » · « the man lowers out of frame while the jar rises into the center ».
